SINE FLU Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 31, 2026

SINE FLU by WHYDUCK STUDIO does not use accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or network services. The app does not collect or transmit personal data.

Information stored on the device

Only the high score and volume preference are stored locally in the app's private storage. This information is never sent off the device and may be removed when the app is deleted.

Third parties and tracking

The app does not share user data with third parties and does not track users or devices. It contains no advertising, analytics, social login, or in-app purchase SDKs.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy answers will be updated together.

Contact

For privacy or app support questions, use the public SINE FLU support form. Do not include personal information in a public request.
